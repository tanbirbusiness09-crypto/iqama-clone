Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is IqamaPrint.com?

IqamaPrint is an online service provider facilitating official Jawazat and government document printouts and status inquiries in Saudi Arabia.

Our average delivery time is 3 hours. Most standard print requests are completed within 1 to 4 hours during working hours.

p>

Yes, all client data and document numbers are encrypted and strictly used only for processing the requested document.

p>

p>
